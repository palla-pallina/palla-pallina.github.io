MediTrack: IoT-Based Smart Medicine Dispenser

MediTrack is a technology-driven medication management system designed to enhance medication safety, dispensing accuracy, operational efficiency, and traceability at Ospital ng Sampaloc. It integrates a cloud-connected platform, an Android-based nurse application, a web-based administration dashboard, and an IoT-enabled smart medicine dispenser to automate medication scheduling, verification, and dispensing. The dispenser uses an ESP32 microcontroller, a carousel mechanism with 10 dedicated medicine compartments, servo-controlled gates, IR or weight sensors, LED indicators, and a secured enclosure to ensure organized medication storage, prevent drug mixing and cross-contamination, and verify successful dispensing.

The system uses Firebase as its centralized cloud backend to synchronize patient records, prescriptions, medication schedules, dispensing logs, and device status updates in real time. Physicians and authorized staff manage patient information and prescriptions through the web dashboard, while nurses use their authorized accounts on the mobile application to access medication schedules. Before dispensing, the system validates the prescribed medication, dosage, patient, and schedule to ensure accurate medication matching. Once verified, the dispenser automatically aligns the appropriate compartment and releases the medication sequentially. All dispensing activities and user actions are recorded for monitoring, reporting, and accountability, supporting a safer and more efficient medication administration workflow.

StKPortal: Web-based Church Management System

StKPortal is a web-based, self-hosted church management system designed to streamline administrative operations and provide a secure digital platform for managing church documents, records, and donations. It aims to reduce reliance on manual procedures by automating essential administrative tasks and implementing an organized file management system. Through its centralized platform, church personnel can manage important information more efficiently while maintaining better organization and accessibility of records.

In addition to internal management, StKPortal provides parishioners with convenient online services, including booking dates for baptisms, weddings, funeral masses, and blessings, submitting prayer requests for mass intentions, making online donations, and accessing church announcements and mass schedules. By integrating administrative tools with accessible digital services, StKPortal seeks to improve church workflows, strengthen the parish’s online presence, and make church services more convenient and accessible to the community.

tintaPH: A Filipino Art and Social Media Platform

tintaPH is a Filipino-centric art social media platform designed to showcase Filipino artists, encourage creative collaboration, and provide networking opportunities for individuals of varying skill levels. It serves as a centralized space where artists can share their work, connect with potential clients and collaborators, and discover opportunities within the local art community. The platform promotes Filipino creativity by providing resources and promotional features that help artists gain visibility and develop their careers.

The platform offers artist profiles where users can showcase portfolios and organize themed artwork collections, as well as categorized galleries for Traditional Art, Digital Art, Photography, and Artisan Crafts to simplify art discovery. Its user forums support discussions, job postings, and employment opportunities, while an interactive event calendar highlights local art exhibitions, workshops, and other creative events. Through these features, tintaPH aims to strengthen the Filipino art community by making artistic expression, professional networking, and collaboration more accessible.
