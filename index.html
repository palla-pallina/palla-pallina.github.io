MediTrack: IoT-Based Smart Medicine Dispenser

MediTrack is a centralized medicine-management ecosystem consisting of an IoT-enabled dispenser, cloud database, web dashboard, and nurse mobile application. It addresses workflows that rely on handwritten prescriptions, paper-based patient records, and multi-step coordination among doctors, pharmacists, and nurses. The system integrates medication scheduling, dispensing, administration, and monitoring.

The solution combines an automatic oral medicine dispenser, web dashboard, Android nurse application, and Firebase cloud database. These components centralize patient and prescription data, validate due medications, provide notifications, control dispensing, and generate activity logs to support safer and more accountable medication management.

Key Features:

  • Automated Medicine Dispensing - Releases prescribed oral medications according to schedules, reducing manual handling.
  • Real-time Medication Notifications - Provides nurses with reminders and alerts for scheduled medication tasks.
  • Patient and Prescription Management - Centralizes patient information, medications, dosages, frequencies, and prescription dates.
  • Inventory Management - Tracks medication stocks, additions, deductions, and adjustments.
  • Medication Administration Logs - Records logins, medication and inventory changes, prescriptions, dispenser activities, and dispensing events.

StKPortal: Web-based Church Management System

StKPortal is a centralized, self-hosted church management system consisting of a web-based administrative platform, digital document management system, online reservation portal, and donation management system. It addresses workflows that rely on manual recordkeeping, physical document storage, and separate processes for managing church operations and parishioner services. The system integrates document management, event scheduling, sacramental reservations, donation tracking, and community engagement.

StKPortal features a web-based interface, organized digital file storage, a centralized database, and accessible online services for church personnel and parishioners. Developed using HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL, the platform simplifies administrative processes, improves information management, and provides convenient access to church services.

Key Features:

  • Digital Document and Records Management - Organizes and manages official church documents, including baptismal and marriage records, through a centralized web interface.
  • Parishioner Information Management - Maintains member profiles, contact information, and related documents with an emphasis on secure information handling.
  • Event and Priest Schedule Management - Supports the planning and tracking of church events, religious ceremonies, community gatherings, outreach programs, and priest schedules.
  • Online Sacramental Reservations - Allows parishioners to reserve dates for baptisms, weddings, funeral services, and other church ceremonies.
  • Reservation and Certificate Tracking - Enables registered users to monitor their sacramental reservations and check certificate pickup availability.
  • Donation Management - Centralizes donation records and supports online contributions through GCash and Metrobank bank transfers.
  • Church Announcements and Mass Schedules - Provides an announcement board for publishing mass schedules, upcoming events, church programs, and important updates.
  • Community Prayer Board - Allows church members to submit and view prayer requests, encouraging participation and community support.

tintaPH: A Filipino Art and Social Media Platform

tintaPH is a Filipino-centric art and social networking platform consisting of a responsive website, mobile application, artist portfolio system, and community networking platform. It addresses the need for a centralized digital space where Filipino artists can showcase their creative work, connect with potential clients and collaborators, and discover professional opportunities. The platform integrates artwork sharing, portfolio management, art discovery, community discussions, and event promotion to support the growth of the local art community.

tintaPH integrates a responsive web platform, an Android mobile application, categorized artwork galleries, user forums, and an interactive event calendar into a unified digital solution. Developed using HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, and Kotlin, the platform showcases Filipino artistic talent, fosters professional networking, and provides accessible opportunities for collaboration and career advancement.

Key Features:

  • Filipino-Centric Art Community - Provides a dedicated platform for Filipino artists to showcase their talents, access creative resources, build professional connections, and promote their work.
  • Artist Profiles and Portfolios - Allows users to showcase their artwork, organize themed collections, and present their creative portfolios to potential clients and collaborators.
  • Categorized Artwork Galleries - Organizes artwork into Traditional Art, Digital Art, Photography, and Artisan Crafts, making it easier for users to browse and discover creative works.
  • Community Forums and Job Opportunities - Enables users to participate in art-related discussions, share ideas, post job openings, and seek employment or collaboration opportunities.
  • Art Event Promotion - Features an interactive event calendar highlighting local art exhibitions, workshops, and other creative activities to help users discover and participate in community events.
